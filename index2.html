Artistic statement

Drink Atlas turns a gallery into a map of what we drink. Every system in this room is driven by data about beer and cocktails, so the light, air and warmth around you change with the drinks themselves. You are not looking at a chart of the data. You are standing inside it.

The six ceiling lights trace where breweries cluster across the United States, each zone brightening with the density of breweries it stands for. The fan follows the strength of the selected drink, so a stiff pour moves more air than a light one. The humidifier recreates the climate a drink comes from, from the damp heat of the tropics to the dry air of the highlands. The heat pump follows how a drink is prepared, warming or cooling the room to match its method.

We usually meet these facts as small print on a label or a menu: alcohol content, place of origin, a line of recipe. Here they become conditions you can feel on your skin. The room draws on brewery locations through our BreweryMap, drink recipes and strengths from TheCocktailDB, and climate data from Open-Meteo.

The room’s systems

Each graph is the last ~85 s of room time measured at that unit: heat pump reads air temperature, humidifier reads relative humidity, fan reads wind speed, each can light reads the illuminance of its own zone.
